Automated reminders

A daily scheduled check (via Render Cron Job) emails members a loan payment reminder 3 days before and on their loan's due date, and a monthly contribution reminder on the 10th to anyone short of the minimum. Requires each member to have an email on file (Members → Edit), and email to be configured (Send Email page shows whether it's set up).
